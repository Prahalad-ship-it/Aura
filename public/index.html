A note about privacy

Your chat is sent to NVIDIA NIM to generate a reply. Your API key stays on this server. Recordings stay in downloads unless you choose to upload one to NVIDIA for analysis or share it.

Type it as a chat message while Aura is open to trigger SOS. It stays in this browser and is not sent to NVIDIA NIM; Aura does not listen in the background. Someone with access to this device may be able to inspect it.

RECORDING REVIEW

Send this recording for analysis?

The audio will be uploaded to NVIDIA for transcription, then the transcript will be sent to the Llama model for a brief summary. Aura does not store the upload; NVIDIA handles it under its own service terms.

Speech recognition can mishear or omit words, and an AI summary can miss context. Only continue if you are comfortable sharing everyone’s recorded speech with NVIDIA.
